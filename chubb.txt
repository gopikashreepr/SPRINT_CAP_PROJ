# Chubb Contractor Onboarding Process

## 1\. Purpose

This document provides a standardized onboarding process for contractors joining Chubb projects, ensuring timely access to systems, tools, project information, and required support contacts.

## 2\. Scope

Applicable to all contractors joining Chubb teams through partner organizations.

---

# 3\. Recruitment and Project Allocation Process

### Interview Process

An interview will be conducted by a Chubb contractor resource from Capgemini. The interview typically focuses on:

- Java
- SQL
- Coding/Programming concepts
- Basic insurance domain knowledge (where applicable)

### Interview Outcome

Upon successful completion of the interview, the interviewer will notify you and connect you with the designated Point of Contact (POC) for onboarding activities.

The POC may request an in\-person meeting to discuss project requirements and onboarding formalities.

Following the discussion, you will receive a project allocation email from **Kachhi Shivam** regarding your assignment to a Chubb project. In some cases, the allocation email may be received before the in\-person discussion.

---

# 4\. Team Introduction and Knowledge Transfer

After project allocation:

- You will be informed about your assigned team and Team Lead.
- Project requirements and expectations will be explained by your POC or Team Lead.
- Knowledge Transfer (KT) sessions will be arranged either virtually or in person.
- Contractors are expected to: 
    - Attend all KT sessions.
    - Maintain detailed notes.
    - Participate actively in discussions.
    - Provide Reverse KT sessions to demonstrate understanding of the application and processes.

---

# 5\. Chubb Office Visit Process

The Chubb office is located at:

**Chubb Office**

Nexity Building

Sattva Knowledge City

Hyderabad

### Office Visit Authorization

Your POC or Team Lead will inform you when an in\-person office visit is required.

Before visiting the office:

1. An OTP\-based visitor access request must be created.
2. Contact:

**Vasanth Kumar R**

Email: vasanth.r\-kumar@capgemini.com

to arrange OTP access.

### Entry Process

- You will receive an OTP via SMS.
- Enter through **Sattva Knowledge City \- Gate 2 only**.
- Show the OTP to the security personnel at the entrance gate.
- Ensure your entry is recorded in the visitor register.
- Proceed to **Octave\-3, 16th Floor**.

### Security Verification

At the Chubb reception/security desk:

- Present the OTP received on your mobile.
- Provide a valid Government\-issued Identity Proof (physical copy or digital copy).
- Present your company laptop for asset verification.
- Be prepared to provide: 
    - Your POC name
    - Team name
    - Project information (if requested)

---

# 6\. VDI (Virtual Desktop Infrastructure) Access Process

## VDI Request Initiation

Your Team Lead will initiate the VDI access request.

For status updates and follow\-up activities, contact:

**Suneel Vuba**

Email: suneel.vuba@capgemini.com

### Additional Information Requirement

A member of the Chubb Onshore Team may contact you to confirm your:

- Last Name expansion
- Personal details required for account setup

---

# 7\. VDI Setup Process

A Chubb support team member will schedule a setup session and assist you with the VDI login process.

The setup assistance is generally provided through:

- Webex Meeting
- Browser\-based screen sharing session

### Required Software

Install the following before the setup session:

**Omnissa Horizon Client**

This can be downloaded from:

- Company Portal

### Pre\-requisites

Before attending the setup call, ensure you have received:

- Chubb Email ID
- LAN ID
- Temporary Password
- VDI Login Instructions

These credentials are required to complete the VDI setup successfully.

---

# 8\. VDI Login Issues

If the VDI ticket has been raised and you are unable to access the environment, contact the Chubb Helpdesk.

### Chubb Business Services India (CBSI)

Phone: 1800\-572\-2274

PIN: 1708

When contacting the helpdesk:

- Provide your VDI Ticket Number.
- Explain the issue encountered during login.
- Request status updates if ticket processing is pending.

---

# 9\. Application Access and Software Requests

After gaining access to the Chubb environment, verify availability of required applications such as:

- Microsoft Teams
- OneNote
- Microsoft Word
- Outlook
- Required project tools and applications

If any required software or access is unavailable:

- Raise a helpdesk ticket.
- Provide application name and access details.
- Follow up until access has been provisioned.
